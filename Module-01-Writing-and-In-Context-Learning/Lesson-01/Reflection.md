# Reflection

## What Changed My Perspective

This lesson changed my understanding of prompt engineering.

Before this lesson, I believed that obtaining high-quality AI-generated writing mainly depended on choosing the right words in a prompt. I now understand that effective prompt engineering is a systematic design process involving context, objectives, constraints, audience, examples, and iterative refinement.

## Key Insight

One of the most valuable lessons I learned is that AI does not naturally understand a person's writing style.

Producing outputs that accurately reflect a user's voice requires carefully engineered prompts and sufficient contextual information. High-quality responses are achieved through prompt design rather than chance.


## Relevance to Physics Education

As someone preparing for a career as a Physics AI Subject Matter Expert, I see direct applications of this concept in Physics education.

Whether creating lesson plans, explaining complex concepts, generating numerical problems, or preparing assessments, the quality of AI-generated content will depend heavily on how clearly I communicate the educational context and instructional objectives.


## Questions for Further Exploration

This lesson also raised several questions that I hope to explore in future modules:

- How can AI consistently reproduce a teacher's writing style?
- Which prompt components have the greatest influence on writing quality?
- How can hallucinations be minimized in educational content?
- How can prompt engineering improve Physics teaching and assessment?

Based on this lesson, I will:

This lesson reinforced one fundamental principle:
