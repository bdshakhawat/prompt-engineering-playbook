## Self-Check Question

> I ask an AI for four ways to assess student understanding of a physics concept, and it tells me Method 2 is the best option. What should I do before accepting that conclusion, and why?

**My answer should reference:** checking whether the AI's "best" verdict accounts for real constraints it wasn't given — such as available grading time, class size, or students' prior exposure to that assessment format — and following up with those specifics if the recommendation might change once real classroom context is included. A generated comparison is a decision-support framework, not a final verdict.
