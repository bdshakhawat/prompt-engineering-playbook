Self-Check Question

I've generated five different ways to teach a physics concept, and I want to evaluate them fairly. What two things should I do before asking the AI to evaluate them, and why?

My answer should reference: (1) generating the evaluation metrics in a separate, independent conversation — without the model having seen the five specific teaching approaches — to avoid the criteria being unconsciously biased toward what's already been generated; and (2) reformatting all five teaching approaches into a consistent structure before evaluation, so that differences in how persuasively or thoroughly each one happens to be written don't influence the outcome more than their actual content does.
