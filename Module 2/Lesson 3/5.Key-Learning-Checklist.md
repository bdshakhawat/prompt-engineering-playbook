Self-Check Question

I ask an AI for three different explanations of a physics concept in one prompt, but they all end up feeling like reworded versions of the same explanation. What are two specific changes I could make to get more genuine diversity?

My answer should reference: (1) switching to independent generation — running the prompt in separate conversations (or different AI tools) rather than requesting all three in a single prompt, so the model can't see and be influenced by the other outputs; and (2) explicitly naming a higher level of variation — such as asking for three different pedagogical approaches or frameworks, rather than just asking for "three different explanations" with no specified dimension of difference.
